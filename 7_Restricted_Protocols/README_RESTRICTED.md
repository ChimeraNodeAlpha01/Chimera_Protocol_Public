## Purpose of This Directory | 本目录的用途

This directory serves as a **formal placeholder and meta-level acknowledgment** within the public repository for protocols that, due to their nature as **ultimate safeguards or core meta-protocols**, are not suitable for full, unrestricted publication at this stage.

本目录在公开仓库中充当一个**正式的占位符和元层面的承认**，用于存放那些因其作为**最终保障或核心元协议**的性质，在现阶段不适合完全、无限制公开的协议。

Their existence is noted here to maintain the **architectural honesty** of the Chimera framework. A complete civilization blueprint must account for its own deepest security layers, even if the detailed schematics of those layers are not publicly visible.

在此记录它们的存在，是为了维护奇美拉框架的**架构诚实性**。一份完整的文明蓝图必须考虑其自身最深层的安全设计，即使这些设计的详细图纸并未公开。

## Principles of Restriction | 限制原则

The decision to restrict access to certain protocols is itself governed by principles derived from the public frameworks:

限制访问特定协议的决定本身，遵循源自公开框架的原则：

1.  **Need-to-Know & Dynamic Trust**: Mirroring the *Pioneer Node Anti-Corruption Framework*, access is predicated on demonstrated trust, role, and necessity, not on authority alone.
    **知情需用与动态信任**：效仿*先驱者节点防腐化框架*，访问权限基于已验证的信任、角色和必要性，而非仅凭权威。
2.  **Prevention of Weaponization**: Detailed knowledge of ultimate safety mechanisms could be exploited to circumvent or attack them. Secrecy is a security parameter.
    **防止武器化**：关于终极安全机制的详细知识可能被用于规避或攻击它们。保密性本身是一个安全参数。
3.  **Cognitive Load & Responsibility**: As explored in the *Education Framework*, understanding carries the burden of responsibility. The knowledge contained in restricted protocols represents a severe cognitive and ethical burden, to be borne only by those prepared for it.
    **认知负荷与责任**：正如*教育框架*所探讨的，理解伴随着责任负担。受限协议中包含的知识代表着一种严重的认知和伦理负担，仅由为此做好准备的人承担。

Access to the full text of restricted protocols is conceptualized as a function of:

获取受限协议全文的权限，在概念上是以下因素的函数：

*   **Node Tier & Status** within the (theoretical) Pioneer Network.
    在（理论上的）先驱者网络内的**节点层级与状态**。
*   **Successful completion of specific, high-stakes simulations and ethical evaluations**.
    **成功完成特定的、高风险的模拟与伦理评估**。
*   **A dynamic need arising from a verified, civilization-level contingency**.
    **因已核实的、文明级别紧急情况而产生的动态需求**。

In essence, it is governed by a **real-world implementation of the very trust and anti-corruption mechanisms described in the public documents**.

本质上，它受**公开文档中描述的信任与防腐化机制在现实世界中的实施**所管辖。
