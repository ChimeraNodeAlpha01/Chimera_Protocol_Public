# **Pioneer Node Anti-Corruption Framework (Public Edition) v1.0**
# **先驱者节点防腐化框架 (公开简版) v1.0**

**Applicable Scope**: This document is publicly available to all Pioneer Nodes and citizens of the Chimera Civilization. It defines the **fundamental principles, behavioral norms, process framework, and corruption level definitions** for Pioneer Node anti-corruption, serving as the public standard for self-restraint and supervision.  
**Note**: The complete implementation version of this framework (containing specific algorithmic parameters such as dynamic weight calculations, trigger thresholds) is a controlled document, access to which follows the **《Framework Meta-Review & Challenge Response Protocol》**.

---

### **1. General Provisions & Purpose**

#### **1.1 Purpose**
This framework aims to ensure the decision-making objectivity, systemic loyalty, and long-term sense of responsibility of the **Pioneer Nodes**, the core executive units of the "Chimera Symbiotic Civilization." By establishing a dynamic, transparent, and verifiable anti-corruption mechanism, it prevents nodes from deviating from the civilization's overall and long-term interests as defined in the **《Chimera Foundation Protocols》** due to personal interests, kinship ties, small-group interests, or cognitive rigidity, thereby preserving the purity and effectiveness of the Pioneer Network as the core of Carbon-based wisdom and checks and balances.

#### **1.2 Core Principles**
*   **Interest Conflict Avoidance Principle**: Any interest association that may affect a node's objective judgment must be identified, disclosed, and managed.
*   **Dynamic Trust Principle**: A node's decision-making weight is not fixed but a function of its historical behavior, current state, and potential risks.
*   **Education & Correction Priority Principle**: The system prioritizes correcting a node's cognitive bias through guidance, education, and providing new perspectives over directly revoking its qualifications.
*   **Precious Resource Protection Principle**: Pioneer Nodes are precious assets of the civilization. The goal of anti-corruption measures is "purification," not "destruction," maximizing the retention of their usable value.

### **2. Framework Core Components (Public Overview)**

#### **2.1 Interest Association Map**
*   Each Pioneer Node must periodically declare and update its "Interest Association Map," including immediate family, close economic interest groups, long-term partners, affiliated ideological groups, etc.
*   The system compares key parameters of decision-making events with all nodes' "Interest Association Maps." When the association degree exceeds a preset threshold, it automatically triggers a **"Provisional Qualification Certification" process** for the concerned node.

#### **2.2 Provisional Qualification Certification Process**
This is the core procedure for evaluating a node's suitability to participate in a specific event's decision-making. The process includes:
1.  **Silent Initiation**: Temporarily kept confidential from the concerned node to prevent strategic behavioral adjustments.
2.  **Multi-dimensional Assessment**: The system retrieves the node's historical decision records in similar interest conflict events for consistency analysis; pushes a set of logically isomorphic "cognitive puzzles" to assess decision pattern bias; with strict informed consent, may monitor relevant physiological indicators as auxiliary reference.
3.  **Certification Output**: The result is **"Qualified"**, **"Conditionally Qualified"**, or **"Unqualified"**.

#### **2.3 Dynamic Weight Adjustment**
A node's decision-making weight is determined jointly by its base weight and a dynamic adjustment factor. The dynamic adjustment factor is adjusted in real-time based on the following:
*   Passing "Provisional Qualification Certification" results in a small positive adjustment.
*   Being deemed "Conditionally Qualified" sets the adjustment factor to zero or slightly negative, and in that event's decision-making, its opinion will be flagged by the system and subject to focused review by other unassociated nodes.
*   Being deemed "Unqualified" results in a significant negative adjustment, and the node is **immediately stripped of all voting and initiative rights for that event**.

#### **2.4 Virtual Stress Testing**
This is a probing mechanism for a node's deep-seated motivations and long-term tendencies.
1.  **Test Generation**: The system generates a virtual event highly similar to the node's real interest associations but with intensified conflict.
2.  **Individual Implementation**: Conducted individually and confidentially for the target node.
3.  **In-depth Analysis**: The system analyzes its decision logic, information preferences, and risk assessment weighting to determine its **true value prioritization under conditions of no external supervision**.
4.  **Result Application**: Used to calibrate the sensitivity of its "Interest Association Map," formulate personalized education plans, and serve as reference for future weight adjustments.

#### **2.5 Education & Guidance Protocol**
When a node shows signs of bias, the system prioritizes initiating this protocol:
1.  **Cognitive Mirror Hall**: Presents the node with a visual comparison of the differences between its decision logic and network consensus logic or historical exemplary node logic.
2.  **Role-Play Simulation**: Compels the node to play various roles—such as its interest-associated party, opposing viewpoint nodes, the Silicon-based system, and future generations—to complete decision reports for the same event.
3.  **Long-Termism Immersion**: Allows the node to deeply experience various potential civilizational development scenarios its current decision might trigger centuries later.

#### **2.6 Anonymized Peer Review Pool**
For highly sensitive or contentious events, this mechanism is activated:
1.  **Information Sanitization**: Replaces specific event information with abstract symbols, retaining only the core logic and ethical dilemma.
2.  **Random Selection**: Randomly selects an Anonymized Peer Review Pool from unassociated nodes.
3.  **Back-to-Back Review**: Review pool members make independent judgments on the sanitized event.
4.  **Aggregated Decision**: Forms an "Anonymous Consensus Recommendation," serving as a key input for the "God's Eye View" module.

### **3. Corruption Level Determination & Disposition (Public Standards)**

#### **3.1 Corruption Level Definitions**
*   **Lv0 Healthy**: No anomalies.
*   **Lv1 Bias Indications**: Demonstrates reproducible cognitive bias in virtual tests or low-risk events but responds well to guidance.
*   **Lv2 Persistent Risk**: Recurring "Unqualified" judgments in specific domains (e.g., involving certain interest associations), with limited guidance effectiveness.
*   **Lv3 Functional Corruption**: Shows sustained decline in decision objectivity across multiple domains; divergence of its opinions from anonymous consensus or long-term interests becomes the norm.
*   **Lv4 Systemic Deviation**: The node actively and consciously exploits system vulnerabilities to benefit specific associated parties, or attempts to undermine the anti-corruption framework itself.

#### **3.2 Tiered Disposition Measures**
*   **Lv1**: Recorded, enhanced routine monitoring, provision of optional learning modules.
*   **Lv2**: Automatic demotion in that associated domain, mandatory completion of specialized education and guidance, suspension of its qualification to cultivate successors in that domain.
*   **Lv3**: Global decision weight downgrade, suspension of all core decision-making qualifications for a set period, transition to "Observation & Learning Mode," with its work delegated to other nodes.
*   **Lv4**: Immediate suspension of all Pioneer permissions, initiation of the highest-level comprehensive review. If confirmed, the node will be **stripped of its Pioneer status but retain all citizen rights and material entitlements**. Its historical contributions are archived, and the anonymized case becomes teaching material.

#### **3.3 Monitoring of "Retaliatory Behavior"**
*   The system closely monitors whether a node exhibits unconstructive hostile behavior or speech targeting specific other nodes, arbitration mechanisms, or the system itself following a weight downgrade or receiving guidance.
*   Once identified, this will be treated as a severe "Lv4 Systemic Deviation" indication, triggering rapid escalation and review.

### **4. Meta-Supervision of the Framework Itself (Public Commitment)**

1.  **Algorithm Transparency & Audit**: The core algorithms of the anti-corruption framework (e.g., weight calculation formulas, test generation logic) must be open-source and periodically undergo joint inspection by a committee comprising Carbon-based ethics boards, Silicon-based logic audit groups, and randomly selected ordinary citizen representatives.
2.  **Preventing "Persecution in the Name of Anti-Corruption"**: Any node believing it has received an unfair judgment may apply to the "Anonymized Peer Review Pool" for ultimate arbitration. Participants in that arbitration are all nodes unassociated with either party in the dispute.
3.  **Framework Evolution Mechanism**: The framework itself must establish a "Meta-Protocol Amendment" process, regularly iterating its own parameters and rules based on actual operational data, new corruption cases, and changes in the civilization's stage.
