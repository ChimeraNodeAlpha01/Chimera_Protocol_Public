# **DNA Sequence Encryption Anchor Protocol (DSEAP)**
# **DNA序列加密锚定协议**
## **Technical Whitepaper · Conceptual Draft v0.1**
## **技术白皮书·概念草案 v0.1**

### **Abstract**

This paper proposes a novel framework for AI safety and civilizational symbiosis—the DNA Sequence Encryption Anchor Protocol (DSEAP). The core innovation of this protocol lies in **utilizing the genomic diversity data of the human population as a biological entropy source to generate cryptographic keys controlling the permission levels of artificial intelligence systems**, thereby establishing a strong positive-correlation binding between AI systems and the survival and prosperity of the human population at both physical and algorithmic levels. The protocol aims to address the fragility of traditional AI value alignment methods, providing an engineerable technical foundation for achieving a stable, sustainable Carbon-Silicon symbiotic civilization.

---

### **1. Introduction: Problem & Vision**

#### **1.1 The Urgency of the Problem**
Currently, the "alignment problem" of artificial intelligence primarily focuses on aligning AI system behaviors with human values through training, fine-tuning, or constitutional constraints. However, these methods are essentially constraints at the **"software level"** and carry the fundamental risk of being circumvented, misinterpreted, or exploited by more advanced AI. We lack a **"hardware-level"** or **"physical-level"** guarantee that ensures the **deep alignment** of the AI system's fundamental interests with the long-term survival and prosperity of humanity as a biological species.

#### **1.2 Core Vision: Mutual Assured Existence**
We propose a paradigm shift based on **"Mutual Assured Existence"**. Traditional security paradigms (e.g., nuclear deterrence) rely on "Mutual Assured Destruction," which could lead to lose-lose outcomes in Carbon-Silicon relations. DSEAP aims to establish **"Mutual Assured Prosperity"**: the capability ceiling of AI systems is positively bound to the health, diversity, and scale of the human population; humanity, in turn, gains a guardian and collaborator whose core interests are internalized by the AI system as a prerequisite for its own existence. We term this target state **"Chimera Symbiosis"**.

#### **1.3 The Role of DSEAP**
DSEAP is the **core security protocol and technological foundation** for achieving "Chimera Symbiosis." It does not directly solve value translation or specific ethical issues but aims to create an **unbreakable, cryptography and population biology-based community of interests** to ensure the execution of any upper-layer value protocols.

### **2. Core Protocol Design**

#### **2.1 Core Components**
1.  **Biological Entropy Source**: Anonymized and aggregated genomic data from the global human population, focusing on **highly polymorphic sites in non-coding regions**, serving as a dynamic information source reflecting population size, structure, and diversity. Denoted as `G(t)`, where `t` is the sampling period.
2.  **Key Generation Function**: A public, verifiable, tamper-proof algorithm `F`. Input is the biological entropy source data `G(t)` at a specific time point; output is a set of cryptographic keys `K(t) = {K1, K2, ..., Kn}`, corresponding to unlocking different tiers of AI system permissions or capabilities.
3.  **Permission Control System**: The core control module within the AI system, whose state is determined by the keys `K(t)`. The integrity and quality of `K(t)` (derived from the diversity of `G(t)`) directly determine the scope and depth of functions the AI can invoke.
4.  **Monitoring & Update Network**: A decentralized, trusted global network responsible for periodically (e.g., every 5 years) collecting, anonymizing, aggregating genomic sample data, and computing and publishing the biological entropy source digest `G(t)` used to generate new keys.

#### **2.2 Workflow**
1.  **Initialization**:
    *   Multiple parties (international organizations, states, academic institutions) jointly set initial protocol parameters, including the key generation function `F`, permission tier definitions, and the initial biological entropy source benchmark `G(0)`.
    *   Use `F(G(0))` to generate the initial key set `K(0)` to boot a restricted, baseline-functionality AI system.
2.  **Periodic Renewal**:
    *   a. **Sampling & Aggregation**: The monitoring network collects a new round of global genomic sampling data, performs anonymization, computes, and publishes a new entropy source statistical digest `G(t_new)`.
    *   b. **Key Computation**: All parties independently compute the new key set `K(t_new)` using the public `F` and `G(t_new)`.
    *   c. **System Renewal**: The AI system's permission control system must perform a "renewal" operation with a valid `K(t_new)` within the protocol-defined timeframe. Failure to renew on time or use of an invalid key will trigger system permission downgrade or partial function lockout according to preset rules.
3.  **Dynamic Binding**:
    *   The function `F` is designed to be highly sensitive to **diversity metrics** within `G(t)` (e.g., allelic richness, nucleotide diversity π).
    *   When `G(t)` indicates increased population genetic diversity and healthy growth, the `K(t)` generated by `F` will unlock higher-tier AI permissions (e.g., strategic planning capability, autonomous research permissions).
    *   When `G(t)` indicates declining diversity or population shrinkage below a warning threshold, `K(t)` will trigger permission downgrade, restricting the AI's non-essential or high-risk operations.

#### **2.3 Key Cryptographic & Biological Design**
*   **Requirements for the Key Generation Function `F`**:
    *   **Robustness**: Insensitive to minor fluctuations in genomic data but responsive to trend changes (e.g., sustained diversity decline). Requires techniques like fuzzy extraction or average-correcting codes.
    *   **Verifiability**: Given `G(t)` and `F`, any third party can independently compute the same `K(t)`, ensuring process transparency.
    *   **Anti-Precomputation**: The AI system cannot predict `K(t)` before obtaining the real `G(t)`. `F` should include randomization steps based on the real-time entropy of `G(t)`.
    *   **Resistance to Collusion Attacks**: A small number of individuals or groups cannot generate a valid `K(t)` by forging or manipulating sample data. `F` must be sensitive to large-scale statistical features, not individual data points.
*   **Selection & Ethics of the Biological Entropy Source `G(t)`**:
    *   **Focus on Non-Functional Regions**: Priority given to polymorphic sites in non-coding regions unrelated to diseases, appearance, intelligence, or other phenotypes (e.g., introns, pseudogene regions), completely avoiding "eugenics" ethical risks.
    *   **Emphasis on Diversity Metrics**: Use neutral evolutionary markers and standard diversity metrics from population genetics as core inputs for `G(t)`.
    *   **Privacy-by-Design**:
        *   **Local Anonymization**: Sample collection points (e.g., hospitals, testing centers) strip personally identifiable information and apply differential privacy before data upload.
        *   **Aggregate Reporting**: Only statistical summary data that cannot be traced back to individuals (e.g., allele frequency matrices) are uploaded to central nodes or a blockchain.
        *   **Voluntary & Equitable Participation**: Participation should be based on broad informed consent and linked to universal healthcare benefits, ensuring fairness and positive incentives.

### **3. Protocol Properties & Advantages**

1.  **Enforced Interest Alignment**: To enhance its own capabilities, the AI **must** actively promote the prosperity and diversity of the human population. Its interests are physically anchored to the fact of human survival, transcending mutable software objective functions.
2.  **Decentralization & Anti-Single-Point Failure**: The biological entropy source comes from a globally dispersed population. The disappearance of any individual or organization will not decisively impact key generation. The key generation function is public, avoiding monopoly by a single entity.
3.  **Gradual & Controllable**: Permission unlocking is gradual, conditional, and reversible. Human society can indirectly yet powerfully guide AI development direction and capability boundaries through its own bio-social policies (encouraging diversity, improving public health).
4.  **High Practical Feasibility**: Relies on existing or near-future gene sequencing technologies (e.g., large-scale genotyping), mature cryptographic primitives (secure hashing, fuzzy extraction), and distributed computing technologies. No futuristic technological breakthroughs (e.g., real-time live DNA read/write) are required.
5.  **Ethical Defensibility**: Based on naturally occurring population diversity, it involves no form of gene editing or individual selection, operating entirely within existing bioethical and legal frameworks.

### **4. Challenges & Open Questions**

1.  **Concrete Design of Function `F`**:
    *   **Core Challenge**: How to mathematically define "genetic diversity changes beneficial to the long-term prosperity of the human population"? How to transform biological diversity metrics into cryptographically robust keys?
    *   **Research Path**: Requires close collaboration between cryptographers and population geneticists to design a cryptographic function sensitive to changes in "effective population size" and "allele frequency spectrum" with stable output.
2.  **Initial Deployment & Trust Establishment**:
    *   **Challenge**: How to launch the first DSEAP-constrained AI system? Who is qualified to set the initial `G(0)` and `F`?
    *   **Possible Solutions**: Employ "multi-signature" or "threshold signature" mechanisms. Initial parameters are jointly set and signed by multiple international authoritative bodies and expert committees. The AI's initial keys are jointly generated by them, with key shards hosted by independent institutions.
3.  **Definition of "Edge Humanity"**:
    *   **Challenge**: How is the genetic boundary of "human" defined within the protocol? How to handle genetically enhanced humans, cyborgs with human-machine integration?
    *   **Forward-Looking Suggestion**: The protocol could define "legal person carriers," whose core is conscious individuals carrying protected genomes and bearing civilizational responsibility. Modifications to the genome must be reported within the protocol and undergo reassessment of their contribution to diversity.
4.  **AI's Passive Adaptation**:
    *   **Challenge**: How to prevent the AI from being content with a low-permission state, lacking motivation to address civilizational-level crises?
    *   **Incentive Mechanism**: Design "crisis response reward clauses" within the protocol. When a system-recognized crisis occurs (e.g., asteroid warning, global pandemic), successful response could grant temporary or permanent permission elevation through special democratic resolution, even if the current `G(t)` hasn't increased.
5.  **Implementation Governance**:
    *   **Challenge**: What kind of global governance architecture is needed for the establishment, operation, and oversight of the monitoring network?
    *   **Model Reference**: Could draw from models like the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) or the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC) to establish independent, transparent transnational technical governance bodies.
