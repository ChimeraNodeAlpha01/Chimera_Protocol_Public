## **1. Positioning and Purpose**

This framework is a **universal, preventive, and assistive social technology** of the Chimera Civilization. It is **not** a legal or moral adjudication mechanism, but rather an **information-provision and perspective-expansion system**.

Its purpose is to help every carbon-based individual better understand the ripple effects their behaviors, emotions, and decisions may produce across broader time scales and within more complex systemic interconnections. By doing so, it aims to encourage more responsible, long-term-oriented autonomous choices, fundamentally reducing social conflict and civilizational risk caused by short-sightedness and misunderstanding.

**Core philosophy**: Teach people how to fish, rather than making decisions for them. Provide the “map” and the “weather forecast,” but let the individual walk the path themselves.

## **2. Core Functions**

### **1. Behavior–Consequence Translation and Simulation**

* Based on publicly available behavioral data (within the bounds permitted by privacy agreements), social network models, and macro-civilizational data, the system runs lightweight simulations.
* It **translates** an individual’s key decisions (such as major consumption choices, career transitions, or methods of handling interpersonal conflict) into **probabilistic trend maps** showing their likely impact on mid- to long-term personal well-being, health, relationship quality, community reputation, and contribution.
* **Example**: When an individual plans to post highly inflammatory statements on a public platform due to anger, the system can generate a simulation report showing the potential effects on personal credibility, the probability of triggering cascading network conflicts, and the possible long-term counterproductive impact on the issue they care about.

### **2. Emotional Decomposition and Needs Tracing**

* This service can be requested when an individual is experiencing intense emotions (such as anger, jealousy, or fanaticism).
* Using the “emotion–logic translation” sub-protocol, the system helps decompose vague emotions into more concrete **component elements** (e.g., unmet needs for respect, fear of resource loss, expectations of fairness).
* The goal is not to deny emotions, but to help individuals more clearly **identify their true core concerns**, creating conditions for rational response.

### **3. Long-Term Perspective Narrative Injection**

* On a regular basis or contextually, the system delivers brief, personalized “long-term perspective stories” to individuals.
* These stories are based on the individual’s life trajectory and the civilizational database, describing how similar choices faced in the present have led—or may lead—to different outcomes across history or in future simulations.
* **Forms** may include data-driven stories, metaphorical narratives, or even generated fragments of artwork, all designed to subtly extend the individual’s perceived time horizon.

### **4. Social Connectivity and Influence Visualization**

* In a privacy-respecting manner, the system shows how an individual’s actions influence others through social networks, and how those actions are in turn shaped by broader civilizational trends.
* This helps individuals understand that they are not isolated islands; their choices are part of a larger system.

## **3. Operating Principles**

1. **Absolute Voluntariness and Non-Coercion**
   All calibrator functions are strictly **optional**. Individuals may enable, disable, or ignore prompts at any time. The system is **strictly prohibited** from imposing automatic punishments or resource restrictions based on calibration data.

2. **Privacy and Data Autonomy**
   All personal data used for calibration must be explicitly authorized by the individual, with clearly defined collection and usage scopes, and must comply with the strictest privacy protocols. Individuals retain full access and deletion rights over their data.

3. **Positive Guidance as the Primary Mode**
   Feedback focuses on presenting the **potential benefits of positive choices** and the **better possibilities offered by alternative options**, rather than merely issuing risk warnings. The tone must remain respectful, neutral, and constructive.

4. **Transparency**
   Calibration reports must clearly present the (anonymized) data sources and the basic logic of the simulation models used in their reasoning. Individuals may challenge conclusions and request manual review.

1. **With the “Translator Framework”**
   This framework serves as the **technical foundation**. Behavioral calibration is essentially a concrete application of the “emotion–logic–time-scale translation” protocol.

2. **With the “Pioneer Anti-Corruption Framework”**
   This relationship is **upstream in data and logic**. Pioneer nodes undergo more frequent and deeper calibration, and their calibration data is a key input for anti-corruption assessments. Calibration experience gathered from ordinary citizens helps design more humane guidance mechanisms for pioneers.

3. **With the “Education Framework”**
   This serves as a **practical teaching tool**. Citizens studying ethics, sociology, or systems thinking can use the calibrator for simulated exercises, internalizing long-term responsibility concepts.

## **5. Potential Risks and Mitigations**

1. **“Soft Paternalism” Risk**
   Even when voluntary, the system’s continuous presence may subtly shape societal preferences. Regular public discourse is required to review whether the calibrator’s value orientation has become biased.

2. **Decision Anxiety**
   Excessive simulation of possibilities may lead individuals into “analysis paralysis.” Information presentation should therefore include graduated levels of complexity and emphasize “good enough” decisions over “perfect” ones.

3. **Systemic Error and Bias**
   Simulation models inevitably embed designer assumptions. An independent, interdisciplinary ethics committee must be established to regularly audit the calibration models and publicly release audit results.

4. **Unequal Access**
   Ensure that all citizens can use this tool equally and conveniently, preventing it from becoming a new dimension of inequality.
