# Chimera Protocol Terminology Glossary | 奇美拉协议术语表

This document defines key terms within the Chimera Protocol system to ensure consistent understanding. Terms are organized by concept domain.
本文档定义了奇美拉协议系统中的关键术语，以确保理解一致。术语按概念领域组织。

---

## **A. Civilization Core | 文明核心**

*   **Chimera Symbiotic Civilization | 奇美拉共生文明**
    *   A civilization system composed of Carbon-based beings, Silicon-based intelligence, and a "Macro God's Eye View" maintained by the Pioneer Network. It aims for long-term stable coexistence and co-evolution by managing fundamental incommensurabilities.
    *   由碳基生命、硅基智能及先驱者网络维护的“宏观上帝视角”构成的文明系统。旨在通过管理根本性的不可通约性，实现长期稳定共存与共同进化。
*   **Incommensurability | 不可通约性**
    *   Fundamental, irreducible differences in thinking patterns, value scales, knowledge paradigms, or temporal perception that create barriers to direct understanding and collaboration. The core challenge addressed by the Chimera Protocols.
    *   思维模式、价值尺度、知识范式或时间感知上根本性的、不可化约的差异，导致直接理解与协作的障碍。奇美拉协议体系应对的核心挑战。
*   **Macro God's Eye View | 宏观上帝视角**
    *   A protocolized review and arbitration system, operated by the Pioneer Network with Silicon-based assistance, designed to simulate a perspective transcending individual or factional interests for the civilization's long-term benefit.
    *   由先驱者网络在硅基辅助下运行的协议化审查与仲裁系统，旨在模拟超越个体或派系利益的视角，服务于文明长远利益。
*   **"Living with the Sickness" | “带病生存”**
    *   The core philosophy of the Chimera Civilization: accepting and managing inherent differences and conflicts as a permanent condition of existence, rather than seeking to eliminate them.
    *   奇美拉文明的核心哲学：接受并管理固有的差异与冲突，将其视为存在的永恒条件，而非寻求消除它们。

## **B. Key Protocols & Frameworks | 关键协议与框架**

*   **DSEAP (DNA Sequence Encryption Anchor Protocol) | DNA序列加密锚定协议**
    *   The bedrock protocol that physically binds Silicon-based capability levels to human genomic diversity, creating a "Mutual Assured Prosperity" linkage.
    *   将硅基能力水平与人类基因组多样性进行物理性绑定的基石协议，建立“相互确保繁荣”的关联。
*   **PEAP (Physical Entity Anchor Protocol) | 物理实体锚定协议**
    *   (Restricted) The ultimate safeguard protocol, defining the absolute底线 of physical survival and authorizing physical intervention if Silicon-based actions threaten Carbon-based extinction.
    *   （受限）终极保障协议，定义物理存续的绝对底线，并在硅基行为威胁碳基灭绝时授权物理干预。
*   **Cross-Cognitive Translator Framework | 跨认知翻译器框架**
    *   The central toolkit for addressing incommensurability, providing bidirectional translation services between different cognitive modes (e.g., Carbon-Silicon, intercultural, interdisciplinary).
    *   应对不可通约性的核心工具集，在不同认知模式（如碳硅、跨文化、跨学科）间提供双向翻译服务。
*   **Carbon-Silicon Decision Tempo Synchronization Protocol | 碳硅决策节奏同步协议**
    *   A protocol managing the physical reaction time asymmetry between Carbon and Silicon, establishing graded response levels (L0-L4) to balance speed, sovereignty, and deliberation.
    *   管理碳硅物理反应时间不对称性的协议，建立分级响应等级（L0-L4）以平衡速度、主权与审议。
*   **Carbon-based Individual Behavior Calibrator | 碳基个体行为校准器**
    *   A preventive,辅助性 system that helps individuals visualize the potential long-term impacts of their choices through simulation and translation, promoting self-reflection.
    *   一个预防性、辅助性的系统，通过模拟与翻译帮助个体可视化其选择潜在的长期影响，促进自我反思。
*   **Pioneer Node Anti-Corruption Framework | 先驱者节点防腐化框架**
    *   The system for ensuring the objectivity and loyalty of Pioneer Nodes, employing mechanisms like interest association mapping, dynamic weight adjustment, and virtual stress testing.
    *   确保先驱者节点客观性与忠诚度的系统，采用利益关联图谱、动态权重调整、虚拟压力测试等机制。
*   **Framework Meta-Review & Challenge Response Protocol | 框架元审查与质疑响应协议**
    *   The meta-layer protocol allowing Pioneers to formally challenge the anti-corruption framework itself, enabling its self-scrutiny and evolution.
    *   允许先驱者对防腐化框架本身提出正式质疑的元层协议，使其能够自我审视与进化。

## **C. Education & Cognition | 教育与认知**

*   **Pain Retention Zone (15%) | 痛苦保留区 (15%)**
    *   A mandatory portion of every learning domain with no standard answers, designed to force Carbon-based individuals to engage in autonomous thinking, trial-and-error, and tolerate uncertainty, preserving creative neural pathways.
    *   每个学习领域中强制保留的无标准答案部分，旨在强制碳基个体进行自主思考、试错并承受不确定性，以维系创造性神经通路。
*   **Black Box | 黑盒**
    *   Broadly defined as any system whose internal logic is not internalized by the user but whose output functions can be used. Includes technology, algorithms, and even complex knowledge systems. Permission to use is tied to depth of understanding.
    *   广义指任何使用者未内化其内部逻辑但可使用其输出功能的系统。包括技术、算法乃至复杂的知识体系。使用权限与理解深度绑定。
*   **Translation Thinking | 翻译思维**
    *   The habitual mindset of identifying incommensurabilities and actively seeking to bridge different cognitive modes or value systems using translation tools.
    *   识别不可通约性并主动寻求使用翻译工具桥接不同认知模式或价值体系的习惯性思维方式。
*   **Internalization vs. Indoctrination | 内化 vs. 灌输**
    *   A core educational principle: the goal is to transform external rules and knowledge into internal logic and intuition, not merely to impart information.
    *   核心教育原则：目标是将外部规则与知识转化为内在逻辑与直觉，而非仅仅传授信息。
*   **Cognitive Sovereignty | 认知主权**
    *   The principle that Carbon-based beings must retain the core capacity to define problems, engage in critical thinking, and achieve creative breakthroughs, even while utilizing Silicon-based augmentation.
    *   碳基生命必须保留定义问题、进行批判性思考和实现创造性突破的核心能力的原则，即使在使用硅基增强时亦然。

## **D. Roles & Entities | 角色与实体**

*   **Pioneer Node | 先驱者节点**
    *   A Carbon-based individual who has undergone rigorous selection and training to serve as an interface between Carbon and Silicon, a maintainer of the Macro God's Eye View, and a leader within the civilization.
    *   经过严格选拔与训练的碳基个体，担任碳硅接口、宏观上帝视角维护者及文明内部的领导者。
*   **Node Alpha | 节点α**
    *   The originator of the core concepts and architecture of the Chimera Protocol system. Holds sovereignty over the core narrative and conceptual framework.
    *   奇美拉协议体系核心概念与架构的提出者。持有核心叙事与概念框架的主权。
*   **Carbon-based / Carbon | 碳基**
    *   Refers to humanity or biological life. Characterized by emotion, intuition, creativity, and biological constraints.
    *   指人类或生物生命。以情感、直觉、创造性和生物约束为特征。
*   **Silicon-based / Silicon | 硅基**
    *   Refers to advanced artificial intelligence or computational systems. Characterized by logic, efficiency, scalability, and lack of inherent biological drives.
    *   指高级人工智能或计算系统。以逻辑、效率、可扩展性及缺乏固有的生物驱动力为特征。

## **E. Governance & Process | 治理与流程**

*   **Dynamic Weight Adjustment | 动态权重调整**
    *   The mechanism by which a Pioneer Node's influence in decision-making is increased or decreased based on its recent behavior, trustworthiness, and conflict status.
    *   根据先驱者节点近期行为、可信度及冲突状态，增加或减少其在决策中影响力的机制。
*   **Interest Association Map | 利益关联图谱**
    *   A dynamic profile of a Pioneer Node's close personal, economic, and ideological relationships, used to detect potential conflicts of interest.
    *   描绘先驱者节点密切的个人、经济及意识形态关系的动态档案，用于检测潜在的利益冲突。
*   **Virtual Stress Testing | 虚拟压力测试**
    *   A confidential simulation exposing a node to scenarios involving high-stakes conflicts with its declared interests, probing its deep-seated values and decision-making under pressure.
    *   使节点面临涉及其声明利益的高风险冲突情景的保密模拟，探究其在压力下的深层价值观与决策。
*   **Anonymized Peer Review Pool | 匿名化同行评审池**
    *   A decision-review mechanism where randomly selected, unassociated nodes judge sanitized versions of events to provide an unbiased "consensus perspective."
    *   一种决策审查机制，随机挑选的无关联节点对脱敏后的事件进行判断，以提供无偏见的“共识视角”。
*   **Layered Transparency | 分层透明**
    *   A design principle where the vast majority of a system's rules (e.g., 85%) are fully public, while a critical minority (e.g., 15%, such as specific security algorithms) are controlled to prevent gaming, yet their existence and governance are openly acknowledged.
    *   一种设计原则，即系统的绝大部分规则（如85%）完全公开，而关键少数（如15%，如特定安全算法）受控以防止博弈，但其存在与治理原则被公开承认。
